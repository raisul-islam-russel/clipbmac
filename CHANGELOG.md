## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-26

### Added
- Clipboard history for macOS (text and images, 50 items, pinned items kept).
- Global hotkey (default `⌘⇧V`; presets `⌥B`, `⌃⌥V`, `⌘⌥V`).
- Auto-paste into the previously active app (Accessibility permission).
- Type-to-search, keyboard navigation, pin and delete.
- Menu-bar app with pause recording, launch at login and clear history.
- Ignores concealed/transient clipboard items from password managers.
- Universal binary build script producing `.app` and `.dmg`.
